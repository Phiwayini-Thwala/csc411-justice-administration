Eswatini Justice Administration System

Police · Judiciary · Correctional Services — three systems, three databases, one process. CSC411 Mini Project, University of Eswatini.

How the three databases communicate

Commands (must succeed, otherwise nothing is saved)Feedback (best effort, can be resent)Live reads (shown in each console)

Justice tracker — find a person in all three systems

Sign in once (an administrator can see all three systems). The same account and token work in every service.

Demo walk-through

  1. Police: process an arrestSign in to the Police Console as police1, choose Process a New Arrest, tick “refer to court”.
  2. Judiciary receives itThe case appears in the Court Console with status Awaiting Hearing. Switch console — no second sign-in.
  3. Court schedules and remandsSign in as court1. Schedule a hearing, then remand: the Custody Console now lists a new inmate.
  4. Police sees the newsBack in the Police Console the case shows Held in Custody; Track shows the live journey.
  5. VerdictDeliver a guilty verdict with imprisonment. The inmate becomes Serving Sentence; the police case closes.
  6. CustodySign in as correctional1. Transfer the inmate; the court file and police case record the new facility.
  7. Sentence servedRelease with Sentence completed: the court case closes and the police case shows the outcome.
  8. AuditSign in as admin1 and open Activity History in each console.
